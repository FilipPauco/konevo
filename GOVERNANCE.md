# Governance

Konevo welcomes long-term collaborators. This document explains how a person
can become more involved and how project decisions are made.

## Current stewardship

Filip Pauco is the current project steward. He is accountable for releases,
security, licensing, commercial arrangements, and repository administration.
Named Product Partners and Core Maintainers share meaningful product and
technical decision-making as described below.

## Paths to greater responsibility

### Community contributor

Anyone may report issues, discuss ideas, fork the repository, and submit pull
requests. Contributions are evaluated on their quality, usefulness, and fit
with the project.

### Trusted contributor

Someone who contributes reliably and collaborates well may be invited to have
Write access. They can work on branches in the repository and review pull
requests, but protected-branch rules still apply and do not grant them GitHub
administrator rights.

### Core Maintainer or Product Partner

A long-term collaborator may be invited to become a Core Maintainer or Product
Partner and own a defined area, such as Inbox, Contacts, integrations,
documentation, or a product initiative. This is a shared-leadership role, not
just a title. They can:

- make day-to-day product and technical decisions in their agreed area;
- jointly shape the roadmap and priorities;
- propose and lead larger initiatives; and
- review contributions and mentor other contributors.

The exact area and access are agreed when the person takes the role. This does
not automatically transfer legal ownership of Konevo or its source code; equal
legal ownership is a separate long-term partnership decision that needs a
written agreement.

## How decisions are made

- Small fixes and documentation changes are normally decided in the relevant
  issue or pull request.
- Product ideas and significant changes should be discussed in an issue or
  discussion before implementation. Product Partners and Core Maintainers are
  part of that decision, especially for their agreed areas.
- A Product Partner or Core Maintainer makes the final day-to-day call within
  their agreed area. Cross-cutting changes should be decided together.
- Security, production deployment, licensing, commercial, and release
  decisions need the project steward's approval because he carries the related
  operational and legal responsibility.
- The aim is consensus. If active project leaders cannot agree on a
  cross-cutting decision, the project steward makes the final call after
  considering the reasoning and project goals.

## Recognition and access

Responsibility is earned through sustained contributions, respectful
collaboration, good judgment, and follow-through. Access is granted only when
it is needed for the role and may be changed if responsibilities change.

Core Maintainers and Product Partners have a real voice in the project; they are
not treated as people who simply implement someone else's decisions.

GitHub Administrator or organization-owner access is reserved for people who
are intentionally becoming equal long-term operational partners. It is not
required to have meaningful product authority or maintainer responsibility.

## Changing this document

Governance can evolve as Konevo grows. Significant changes should be proposed
publicly and discussed with active maintainers before adoption.
